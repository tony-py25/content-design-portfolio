# Content Strategy Example

## Project: AfriLang AI

**Role:** Content Designer / Researcher

**Project type:** AI data marketplace concept

---

## Objective

The objective of the content strategy is to make AfriLang AI understandable and easy to navigate for two main audiences:

1. African-language contributors
2. AI companies and researchers looking for language datasets

The strategy ensures that users receive the right information at the right stage of their journey.

---

# Audience Needs

## Contributors

Contributors need clear answers to:

- What is AfriLang AI?
- What type of language data can I contribute?
- How does the contribution process work?
- What information do I need to provide?
- How will my contribution be handled?
- What happens after I submit my data?

## AI Companies and Researchers

They need to know:

- What datasets are available?
- Which languages are covered?
- What type of data does each dataset contain?
- What can the data be used for?
- How has quality been assessed?
- How can they request or license a dataset?

---

# Content Goals

The content should:

- Explain the platform clearly
- Help users find relevant information quickly
- Reduce confusion around technical terminology
- Guide users toward appropriate actions
- Establish trust
- Maintain consistent terminology
- Support both technical and non-technical audiences

---

# Content Hierarchy

The proposed hierarchy is:

### Level 1 — Core Information

- What AfriLang AI is
- Who it is for
- What problem it addresses
- What users can do

### Level 2 — Supporting Information

- Available datasets
- Contributor requirements
- Quality information
- How the platform works
- Frequently asked questions

### Level 3 — Detailed Information

- Dataset specifications
- Licensing information
- Data-use information
- Technical documentation
- Policies

This hierarchy prevents users from being overwhelmed with technical information before they understand the basic purpose of the platform.
