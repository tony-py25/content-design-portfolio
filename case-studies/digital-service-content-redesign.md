Digital Service Content Redesign

Project Overview

This case study demonstrates how I approach improving digital-service content so that users can understand what a service does, what action they need to take, and what information they need before proceeding.

The focus is on making content clearer, more structured, user-friendly, and easier to navigate.

The Problem

Digital-service users can struggle when information is presented using:

- Long or complicated instructions
- Unclear headings
- Technical language
- Poorly structured information
- Calls to action that do not clearly explain the next step

These problems can increase confusion and make it harder for users to complete a task successfully.

My Content Approach

I would begin by identifying the user's primary goal and the information required to complete the task.

The content would then be reorganized around the user's journey:

1. Understand the service
2. Determine whether the service applies to them
3. Identify the requirements
4. Complete the necessary steps
5. Understand what happens next

Example Before

«Users should carefully read all applicable information and ensure that they have provided the required documentation before proceeding with the application process.»

Improved Version

Before You Apply

Make sure you have all the required documents before starting your application.

You will need:

- A valid identification document
- Your contact information
- Any supporting documents listed in the requirements

Next Step

Review the requirements and prepare your documents before starting the application.

Content Design Principles Used

Clarity

Use simple, direct language and avoid unnecessary jargon.

Scannability

Use headings, short paragraphs, bullet points, and clear calls to action.

User Focus

Organize information around what users need to accomplish rather than around internal organizational structures.

Consistency

Use consistent terminology, formatting, and instructions throughout the service.

Accessibility

Write content that is understandable to users with different levels of digital experience.

Expected Outcome

A clearer content structure can help users:

- Find important information faster
- Understand requirements more easily
- Know what action to take next
- Reduce avoidable mistakes
- Complete digital tasks with greater confidence

My Role

I approached this project from a content-design perspective, focusing on research, information structure, plain-language writing, user needs, and actionable content.

This demonstrates my ability to transform complex information into clear, practical digital content.
