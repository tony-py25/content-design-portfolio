# AfriLang AI — African Language AI Data Marketplace

**Role:** Project Lead, Researcher & Content Designer  
**Project Stage:** Prototype / MVP development  
**Focus:** Content Design • UX Writing • Research • AI • African Languages

---

## Overview

AfriLang AI is a proposed African-language AI data marketplace designed to connect African-language contributors with organizations developing artificial intelligence systems.

The initial focus is **Kiswahili**, with the long-term goal of supporting additional African languages.

The platform is designed around a simple idea:

> Make high-quality African-language data easier to discover, understand, license, and use.

---

## The Problem

Many AI systems require large amounts of high-quality language data for applications such as:

- Speech recognition
- Machine translation
- Conversational AI
- Voice assistants
- Generative AI
- AI evaluation
- Natural-language processing

African languages and regional varieties can be underrepresented in these systems.

At the same time, potential language contributors may have valuable linguistic knowledge but lack an organized way to participate in AI data projects.

AfriLang AI explores how a digital marketplace could connect these two groups.

---

# Users

The platform is designed around several primary user groups.

### AI Companies & Developers

They need access to reliable African-language datasets for AI development and evaluation.

### Language Contributors

Speakers and language professionals can contribute recordings, transcriptions, translations, text, and other language data.

### Researchers

Researchers may need African-language datasets for linguistic and AI research.

### Platform Administrators

Administrators need tools for reviewing submissions, maintaining quality standards, and managing the marketplace.

---

# Content Design Challenge

The challenge was not simply to create written content.

The larger challenge was:

**How can complex AI-data concepts be presented in a way that ordinary contributors, researchers, and technology companies can understand and navigate?**

This required thinking about:

- What information users need
- How information should be organized
- What terminology should be used
- How users move through the platform
- How to describe datasets clearly
- How to communicate trust and quality
- How to reduce confusion around technical concepts

---

# My Content Design Approach

## 1. Research

I researched the relationship between African languages, AI development, language datasets, data collection, transcription, translation, and human evaluation.

I also considered the different information needs of contributors and AI-data buyers.

---

## 2. Define the Users

I separated the platform's audiences according to their goals.

A contributor needs to understand:

**What can I contribute?**

**How will the process work?**

**What information is required from me?**

An AI company needs to understand:

**What datasets are available?**

**What does each dataset contain?**

**What quality controls are used?**

**How can the dataset be licensed or requested?**

This distinction helped shape the content architecture.
